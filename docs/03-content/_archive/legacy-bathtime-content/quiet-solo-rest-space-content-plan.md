# Quiet Solo Rest Space Content Plan

## Working Title

조용히 혼자 쉬기 좋은 공간의 조건

## Strategic Role

This content closes the first spot-guide series and opens the next editorial phase.

The current series has been about why good outside Bathtime spaces are hard to judge.

1. 좋은 사우나를 찾는 일이 생각보다 어려운 이유
2. 호텔 사우나는 왜 아무나 들어갈 수 없을까?
3. 조용히 혼자 쉬기 좋은 공간의 조건

The third article should not merely add another checklist. It should define the criteria Bathtime will use when it later introduces theme-based candidate places.

## Series Decision

Close the current series after this article.

Reason:

- The first article defines the problem.
- The second article shows one operating structure: hotel sauna access.
- The third article turns the previous two articles into reusable criteria.

After that, continuing the same series would make it too broad. The next phase should start a new series around actual theme-based candidate guides.

## Why Not Start With Hotel Sauna Candidate Lists

Hotel sauna candidate guides may bring search traffic, but they are operationally risky.

Risks:

- access policies change
- external use is often unclear
- many candidates require phone confirmation
- expensive hotel framing may narrow the brand
- it can look like a hotel recommendation list

Hotel sauna should return later as a tightly verified candidate guide.

## Why Not Start With Bathtub Stay

Bathtub-stay content is commercially useful, but it shifts the audience lens from `solo-quiet` to `couple-bathtub-stay`.

It should begin a separate lodging-focused series after the solo-quiet foundation is complete.
