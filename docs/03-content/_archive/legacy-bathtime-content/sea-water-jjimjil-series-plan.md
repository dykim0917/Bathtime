# Sea Water Jjimjil Series Plan

## Series Name

주제별 바스타임 공간 후보

## Theme

해수찜질

## Strategic Role

This is the first theme-based place series after `바스타임이 공간을 보는 기준`.

The previous series built Bathtime's criteria:

- 좋은 공간은 사진과 이름만으로 판단하기 어렵다.
- 출입 조건과 운영 구조를 읽어야 한다.
- 조용히 혼자 쉬기 좋은 공간은 출입 조건, 동선, 휴게공간, 소음원, 밀도, 체류 방식, 정보 확인성이 함께 맞아야 한다.

This new series applies those criteria to a concrete theme.

## Why Sea Water Jjimjil First

해수찜질 is a strong first theme because:

- it is culturally distinctive
- it can become both a reading/culture topic and a candidate-guide topic
- it is broader than hotel sauna content
- it can support search-driven entry without becoming a generic ranking post
- it forces Bathtime to practice source transparency, seasonal operation checks, and candidate uncertainty

## Three-Content Set

### 1. Culture Note

Title:

`해수찜질은 어떤 목욕 문화인가`

Role:

Introduce the concept, sensory frame, and cultural context without recommending places.

Primary job:

- explain what readers usually mean by 해수찜질
- separate culture/context from medical or wellness claims
- make readers curious about the place type
- prepare the criteria article

Research required:

High.

Need source-backed explanation of terminology, regional context, public tourism information, and facility-type differences.

### 2. Spot Guide

Title:

`해수찜질 장소를 찾을 때 확인할 것들`

Role:

Define how to evaluate a 해수찜질 place before visiting.

Primary job:

- access and operation checks
- seasonal/closure checks
- facility composition checks
- solo/family/group suitability
- source trust boundaries

Research required:

High.

Need official pages, local tourism pages, map/platform listings, and review-pattern checks. Do not recommend places.

### 3. Candidate Guide

Title:

`전국에서 해수찜질을 찾아볼 때 먼저 확인할 후보들`

Role:

Introduce actual candidate places or regions as `확인 후보`, not recommendations.

Primary job:

- group candidates by region or type
- record source status and last checked dates
- mark missing fields
- show what must be confirmed before visiting
- create search-entry value while preserving caution

Research required:

Very high.

This article must not start production until the candidate research pack exists.

## Recommended Order

1. Publish or draft the Culture Note first.
2. Draft the Spot Guide after terminology and facility types are clear.
3. Research the Candidate Guide last and only after the verification schema is fixed.

## Candidate Guide Guardrail

The candidate guide must not say:

- 전국 해수찜질 추천
- 베스트
- 꼭 가야 할
- 현지인이 추천한
- 직접 다녀온

Use instead:

- 후보
- 확인할 곳
- 살펴볼 지역
- 마지막 확인 날짜
- 공식 정보
- 지도/예약 정보
- 후기 패턴
- 확인 필요
